🌱 SatFarm

SatFarm is a personal field-monitoring tool for farms. It uses free Sentinel-2 satellite imagery and public weather data to track crop health, canopy moisture and rainfall for individual fields, and puts the results on a web dashboard.

What SatFarm does

  • Shows vegetation indices (NDVI, NDRE, NDMI and a combined health score) for each field, mapped and charted over time.
  • Shows rain, temperature, water balance and growing degree days for each field's location.
  • Builds a field report with maps and charts, which the owner can email to the farmer who manages that field.

How SatFarm uses Google

SatFarm signs in to its owner's Gmail account only to create drafts and send those field reports from that account. It does not read, search or store any email. See the privacy policy for details.
